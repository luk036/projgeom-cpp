Detailed Description

A conic section represented by a symmetric 3x3 matrix.

A point \(\mathbf{x}=(x:y:z)\) lies on the conic iff \(\mathbf{x}^T Q \mathbf{x}=0\).

auto fun::Conic::intersect ( const PgLine & line) const->std::vector< PgPoint >
inlinenodiscard

Intersection of a line with the conic.

The line is parametrized as \(\mathbf{x}(t) = u + t\,v\) and substituted into \(\mathbf{x}^T Q \mathbf{x} = 0\), giving a quadratic in \(t\). When its discriminant is a perfect square the roots are rational and the intersection points are returned exactly.

Parameters
[in]lineThe line.
Returns
std::vector<PgPoint> The intersection points (empty if the line does not meet the conic in real points).
Exceptions
std::domain_errorif the intersection is irrational, or the line lies on the conic.

◆ pole()

auto fun::Conic::pole ( const PgLine & line) const->PgPoint
inlineconstexpr

Pole of a line with respect to the conic.

\[ \mathbf{x} = Q^{-1} \mathbf{l} = \frac{\operatorname{adj}(Q)\, \mathbf{l}}{\det Q} \]

The scalar \(1/\det Q\) is dropped because the result is a homogeneous point, so the computation stays exact over the rationals.

Parameters
[in]lineThe line.
Returns
PgPoint
Exceptions
std::domain_errorif the conic is degenerate ( \(\det Q = 0\)).
