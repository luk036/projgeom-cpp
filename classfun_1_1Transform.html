Detailed Description

A 3×3 projective transformation matrix.

Operates on homogeneous coordinates \((x:y:z)\). Supports translation, rotation, scaling, shear, composition, and inverse.

constexpr auto fun::Transform::rotation_from_tangent ( const Frac & t) ->Transform
inlinestaticconstexpr

Rotation from the tangent of its half-angle.

For a rational \(t = \tan(\theta/2)\) this yields an exact rotation through \(\theta\) with rational entries:

\[ \cos\theta = \frac{1 - t^2}{1 + t^2}, \qquad \sin\theta = \frac{2 t}{1 + t^2}. \]
